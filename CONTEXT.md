# finello

A local-only macOS calendar for planning social media posts. She plans what to
post and on which day, adapts the wording per platform, and ticks each one off
once she has published it herself. finello never talks to a social network.

## Language

**Post**:
One planned piece of content on one day. Owns the date, the media, and the
platforms it is going to.
_Avoid_: Entry, Item, Content

**Variant**:
One platform's version of a Post — its caption, description, hashtags, and its
own done-checkmark. A Variant carries no media and no date of its own.
_Avoid_: Cut, Version, Placement, Adaptation

**Platform**:
One of the five places she publishes: Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Snapchat,
LinkedIn. She chooses which platforms a given Post targets.
_Avoid_: Channel, Network, Social

**Media**:
The photos, videos, or audio attached to a Post, in the order she wants them.
Shared by every Variant of that Post — the same file goes to every platform.
_Avoid_: Asset, Attachment, File

**Library**:
finello's own store of Media. Media is copied in when she adds it, and finello
owns the copy from then on.
_Avoid_: Vault, Store, Archive

**Done**:
She published a Variant herself, by hand. A Post is *none*, *some*, or *all*
done, derived from its Variants.
_Avoid_: Published, Posted, Complete

**Overdue**:
A Post whose day has passed while at least one of its Variants is still not
Done. finello flags it and never moves it.
_Avoid_: Late, Missed, Backlog

**Schedule**:
To place a Post on a day in the calendar. It never means "publish
automatically at a set time" — finello has no such power and no plans to.
_Avoid_: using "schedule" for automated publishing
