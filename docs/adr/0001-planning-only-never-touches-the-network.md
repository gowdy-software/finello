# finello plans posts; it never publishes them

finello is a planning calendar, not a publishing engine: it has no network
access at all, and the user publishes every post by hand and then ticks it off.
The alternative — actually posting to Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Snapchat and
LinkedIn on a schedule — means five OAuth flows, five developer accounts, and
platform app reviews that a stranger can refuse or revoke, all of it breaking
silently and permanently thereafter. finello is a gift for one person; a tool
that cannot break is worth more here than a tool that saves five minutes a day.

## Consequences

- There is no such thing as engagement, reach, or follower data in finello, and
  there never can be. Any feature that would need it is out of scope by
  construction.
- **Done** means "she published it herself", not "we published it". The
  checkmark is the entire publishing mechanism.
- Nothing she puts into finello ever leaves her Mac, which is why the app needs
  no accounts, no privacy policy, and no security review.
