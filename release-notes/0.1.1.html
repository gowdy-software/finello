Welcome to finello

A calendar for planning what you post, and when. It lives entirely on your Mac — finello never connects to Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Snapchat or LinkedIn, and nothing you put in it is uploaded anywhere.

Planning a post

  • Drop photos, video or audio straight onto a day, or click a day to start something. finello keeps its own copy, so moving or deleting the original later never breaks your calendar.
  • Pick which platforms it is going to. Each one gets its own tab with its own caption, description and hashtags — write the long version once, then use Copy from… and cut it down for the others.
  • Plan a day before the footage exists. A post can be nothing but an idea and a date.

Keeping track

  • Tick each platform off once you have published it. A day shows how far along it is — 1/3 until it is all out, then green.
  • If a day passes with something still to post, finello flags it and counts it in the toolbar. It never moves your posts for you.
  • Drag a post to a different day to reschedule it. ⌘Z undoes it if you did not mean to.

Two views

  • Month for seeing everything at a glance — each day shows its picture.
  • Week for actually writing, with room for captions and tick boxes.

Your media lives in finello's own library. You can always find it at File ▸ Reveal Library in Finder.
